01 / The experience

Intelligence should be useful beyond the answer.

DragonZpyder begins with the fundamentals: conversations that continue, context you can bring with you, and a clear boundary between asking for help and allowing a tool to act.

01

Continue the conversation.

Return to a previous Personal conversation and pick up the thread. The interface is built around continuity rather than treating every interaction as a reset.

02

Work with what you connect.

Connected capabilities are made available through your authenticated Personal scope. The browser does not collect provider secrets or grant itself Workspace access.

03

Approve what matters.

For sensitive actions, inspect the exact proposed step before approving it. If an external result is uncertain, the system should say so rather than pretending the action succeeded.

02 / The engineering

One interface. Clear authority.

DragonZpyder is the experience you use. Operly provides authentication, permissions, approvals and the execution kernel behind it. That separation is deliberate.

What you ask for is not automatically permission to do anything on your behalf.

04 / Looking forward

A wider web of intelligence.

Different models, creative tools and services should be able to work together without forcing people to fragment their work or lose sight of their spending. That is the long-term direction—not a claim that every integration already exists.

Planned

AI Mesh

A model-agnostic interface with bounded delegation to compatible specialist systems, visible usage and cost controls. Model providers and creative services would participate through supported integrations.

Research

Project IC

An auditable usage and accounting layer for consenting AI-service partners. A shared wallet, transferable third-party credits and compute-backed currency do not exist as released DragonZpyder products.

Principle

Build for everyone.

Access to useful intelligence should not depend on technical fluency, a single language, or buying several expensive standalone subscriptions. Practical products come first.
