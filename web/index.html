01 / Why DragonZpyder

We don't need to rebuild every breakthrough.
We need to connect them.

No company can—or needs to—make every exceptional model, creative engine and specialised application. The industry is generating remarkable individual capabilities. But people still move manually between accounts, interfaces, files and separate bills. DragonZpyder's thesis is that connecting those capabilities can unlock more agency than leaving each one isolated.

01 / Choice

Use the right intelligence.

A reasoning system for analysis, a coding model for implementation, a creative model for video. The task should determine the tool, not the brand you happened to subscribe to.

02 / Connection

Turn isolated results into work.

A useful outcome may span research, writing, design, media and specialised apps. Coordinate permitted systems instead of copy-pasting between disconnected products.

03 / Access

Pay for what you use.

Under the planned AIC model, one account would meter usage across participating services. Low-volume experimentation should not require separate subscriptions everywhere.

02 / AI Mesh · Planned

The dots are there.
We build the connections.

AI Mesh is the proposed independent coordination layer. It's not a claim that every AI provider has agreed to join: each connection requires a permitted API or partner arrangement, and every capability retains its own constraints.

01

Find and select

Understand which supported models and applications can handle a task, what they cost and what permissions they require.

02

Delegate and combine

With explicit bounds, one service can produce inputs for another: research into scripts, scripts into scenes, scenes into video, and audio into a finished experience.

03

Keep control

Maintain context, view activity and estimated usage, impose spending ceilings, and approve meaningful external actions before execution.

Illustrative creative chain

Research → story concept → screenplay → visual generation → voice → edit. Different specialist services could contribute to one creative outcome wherever the integrations and rights allow.

03 / Project IC · AIC · Research

One budget.
More possibilities.

Imagine adding $20 to DragonZpyder, then using small portions for a coding task, a video generation, a voice clip or a story experience—without first committing to a separate monthly subscription for every supported service. That's the idea behind Project IC, or AIC (AI Credits).

Participating applications would price their own usage; DragonZpyder would show the amount before charging a common balance. You control where the budget goes.

Research concept only. No universal wallet, issued AIC currency or partner settlement network is live. Existing third-party subscriptions and credits cannot be transferred automatically. Future use requires legitimate API access, commercial agreements and suitable payment compliance.
