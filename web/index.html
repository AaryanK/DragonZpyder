Project IC · Research vision

One balance. More possibilities.

Too often AI spending is fragmented into subscriptions, minimum purchases and unused credits locked inside separate apps. Project IC explores a transparent spending network: one balance accepted by participating AI services, from models and creators to partner storytelling experiences. Partners choose their usage prices; users pay for what they actually want. Existing balances at unrelated providers cannot transfer without those providers' cooperation.

IC

Portable credits

A proposed common accounting layer for participating services, with auditable usage and partner settlement.

02

Pay per experience

Small bundles, metered access and subscriptions can coexist instead of forcing everyone into the same commitment.

03

Compute and value

Long-term research examines GPU-hours, computational operations and verified useful work as service-backed value—not an already issued currency.

Project IC is exploratory. No universal wallet, monetary instrument, third-party settlement network or compute-backed currency is being offered on this page.
