## Full description

DragonZpyder Beta brings your personal AI conversations to Android.

Ask questions, explore ideas, draft writing, and continue conversations in one app. The native Android experience is designed around a personal account and a simple chat interface.

Features in this beta:
- Chat with DragonZpyder through your signed-in account.
- Return to prior conversations where supported by your account.
- Select available AI experiences and models.
- Attach supported content to requests where available.
- Use your device's speech-recognition or text-to-speech capabilities where supported.
- Choose a light or dark appearance.

This is an early-access beta. Some capabilities may be unavailable or change while we validate reliability, security, and the Android experience. Access to connected services depends on the permissions granted to your account. A personal login does not automatically grant access to business workspaces.

DragonZpyder requires an internet connection and an account to use its authenticated features. AI-generated responses may be inaccurate; verify important information independently.
